📌 Campus Log: Day One at IIT ROORKEE

A New Chapter: First Impressions of Campus Life

September 17, 2026

My first day as a student at IIT Roorkee felt like stepping into an ecosystem already alive with energy. Touring the entire campus for the very first time with my parents was truly a memorable experience. Exploring the vibrant greenery, the historical age old architecture and up to date modern technologies truly filled me up with pride. I remember being astonished by the Param Ganga Supercomputer and the new maths and humanities building.

Beneath the Thomason Arches, every conversation carried a spark: ideas about the future, late-night chats with hostel roommates, and the collective ambition to build things that matter.

The campus made the future feel tangible. Between finding my way through unfamiliar corridors and meeting classmates from completely different backgrounds, I realized that college is more than a technical discipline. It is a space for learning to listen closely to the world, then shape what comes next. I truly didn't expect to make these many new friends so quickly. The discussions regarding our dream trips and how we all wanted to live the hostel campus life to the fullest had already started.
